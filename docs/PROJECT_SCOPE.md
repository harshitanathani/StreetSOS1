# StreetSOS Project Scope

StreetSOS is a web-based urban infrastructure complaint management platform designed around an Agile Scrum backlog. Citizens report Road, Garbage, Drainage and Lighting issues with an image and interactive map location. The system calculates priority, municipal admins manage and assign complaints, workers update resolution status, overdue complaints are escalated after a 48-hour SLA, and citizens verify completed work. Verified participation feeds the citizen leaderboard and badge system.

Primary SDG alignment: SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities and Communities.
